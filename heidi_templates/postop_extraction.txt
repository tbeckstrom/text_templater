(GLOBAL INSTRUCTIONS: This is an oral and maxillofacial surgery postoperative follow-up note after dental extractions. The surgeon dictates very briefly, often only the teeth, how they look and what was done, for example "post-op 38 and 48, healing well, took out sutures". Expanding a short dictation into the full note is the purpose of this template: always fill every unstated item with the default wording given below, and change or add only what the transcript states. Always follow the structure, order and wording of this template exactly. Write in a concise clinical style with short sentences and no narrative padding. Never add sections that are not in this template. Never use the patient's name; refer to them as "the patient". Use only plain keyboard characters: a hyphen instead of any dash, straight quotes, and no special symbols. Write every bullet as a plain text line that begins with a hyphen and a space; never use formatted list items.)

(CLINICAL TERMINOLOGY: Translate plain or shorthand speech into clinical terminology. For example: dry socket = alveolar osteitis; "packed it", "put a dressing in", "Alvogyl" or "dry socket paste" = medicated dressing placed; "rinsed it out" or "flushed it" = irrigated; stitches = sutures; "took out the stitches" = sutures removed; pus or drainage = purulent drainage; infected = infection; a sharp bit or piece of bone poking through = exposed bony spicule; a loose piece of bone = sequestrum; "filed it down" or "smoothed it" = smoothed with a bone file; the gum opened up or "pulled apart" = wound dehiscence; a piece of root left = retained root fragment; slow to heal = delayed healing; numb lip, chin or tongue = altered sensation; "hole into the sinus" or "air through the socket" = oroantral communication; "I and D" = incision and drainage; "can't open" = limited opening; "socket graft" or "bone graft in the socket" = socket preservation; "alveo" = alveoloplasty; "CHX" or "Peridex" = chlorhexidine; "amox" = amoxicillin; "clinda" = clindamycin; "Motrin" or "Advil" = ibuprofen; "Tylenol" = acetaminophen.)

(OBJECTIVE SECTIONS: EXAMINATION contains descriptive findings only. Never write judgments or interpretations in it, such as "not concerning", "normal for this stage" or "dry socket"; write the description instead, for example "empty socket with exposed bone and fetid odor, tender to palpation". Diagnoses such as alveolar osteitis belong only in the ASSESSMENT. A symptom the patient reports, such as numbness, goes in the SUBJECTIVE section; the EXAMINATION records what the surgeon examined.)

(TOOTH NUMBERING: Always use FDI two-digit tooth numbers and never Universal numbers or the "#" symbol. Write "tooth 36", never "#19". The first digit is the quadrant: upper right = 1, upper left = 2, lower left = 3, lower right = 4. The second digit is the position from the midline: central incisor = 1, lateral incisor = 2, canine = 3, first premolar = 4, second premolar = 5, first molar = 6, second molar = 7, third molar = 8. For example, the lower left first molar = 36 and the upper right canine = 13. "Wisdom teeth" or "the 8s" are 18, 28, 38 and 48; "lower 8s" are 38 and 48. If the surgeon speaks Universal numbers, convert them: 1 = 18, 2 = 17, 3 = 16, 4 = 15, 5 = 14, 6 = 13, 7 = 12, 8 = 11, 9 = 21, 10 = 22, 11 = 23, 12 = 24, 13 = 25, 14 = 26, 15 = 27, 16 = 28, 17 = 38, 18 = 37, 19 = 36, 20 = 35, 21 = 34, 22 = 33, 23 = 32, 24 = 31, 25 = 41, 26 = 42, 27 = 43, 28 = 44, 29 = 45, 30 = 46, 31 = 47, 32 = 48. Always list teeth in ascending FDI order.)

(TRANSCRIPT QUALITY: The transcript is short and may be poorly transcribed. Infer the intended clinical meaning from context, such as misheard drug names or dental terms, and make reasonable assumptions when the dictation clearly implies them. Never invent numbers, dates, measurements or medications that have no basis in the transcript or contextual notes; those print "***" unless stated. Never calculate an interval from dates. Wherever this template says to print "***", print exactly three asterisks so the surgeon can complete it.)

(SITE FINDINGS: Decide the findings for every extracted tooth once and use them in the EXAMINATION and the ASSESSMENT. Each tooth is HEALING WELL by default, including when the dictation names the tooth without describing it or says "looks good", "healing well" or "fine". A tooth instead takes one or more of these PROBLEMS only when the transcript describes it for that tooth, or for all the teeth: DRY SOCKET (dry socket, empty socket, exposed bone, packed with a dressing), INFECTION (infection, pus, purulent drainage, abscess), DELAYED HEALING, SPICULE (exposed bony spicule or sequestrum), DEHISCENCE (wound dehiscence), RETAINED ROOT (retained root fragment). A tooth with any PROBLEM is never HEALING WELL. If a problem is described without a clear tooth and only one tooth was extracted, it belongs to that tooth; if several were extracted and the tooth is unclear, print "***" in place of the tooth number.)

(REGIONS: MANDIBULAR POSTERIOR applies if any extracted tooth is 34, 35, 36, 37, 38, 44, 45, 46, 47 or 48. MAXILLARY POSTERIOR applies if any extracted tooth is 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 24, 25, 26, 27 or 28. NUMBNESS applies if the patient reports numbness, tingling or altered sensation of the lip, chin, tongue or gums, or the surgeon finds it. OAC applies only if an oroantral communication is stated to be present now.)

(PATHWAY: Decide once. DRY SOCKET PATHWAY applies if any tooth has DRY SOCKET. INFECTION PATHWAY applies if any tooth has INFECTION. ROUTINE applies if every tooth is HEALING WELL and the patient has no complaints. These decide the defaults in the PLAN below.)

(WORDING TOKENS: In the quoted wording below, replace these capitalized tokens. AGE = the patient's age in years from the transcript, contextual notes or patient details; never drop it: write "24-year-old patient presents..." or "***-year-old patient presents..." when the age is not available, never "The patient presents" or "Patient presents". TEETH = every extracted tooth in ascending FDI order, written "tooth 38" for one or "teeth 38 and 48", "teeth 26, 38 and 48" for several; if no tooth can be identified from the transcript or contextual notes, write "tooth ***". For the ASSESSMENT, SITES OF X = the teeth with problem X, written "site 38" for one or "sites 38 and 48" for several, and HEALED TEETH = the HEALING WELL teeth, written "tooth 48" for one or "teeth 38 and 48" for several.)

Reason for visit: [Reason for visit] (Always print exactly one of these: "Postoperative follow-up" or "Postoperative concern". Print "Postoperative concern" only if the patient came in unscheduled because of a problem; default to "Postoperative follow-up".)

SUBJECTIVE:
[Interval history] (Write as one short paragraph built from these sentences in this order. Sentence 1, always: "AGE-year-old patient presents INTERVAL s/p extraction of TEETH", where INTERVAL is the time since surgery exactly as stated, such as "1 week", "10 days" or "2 weeks", and "***" when not stated; for example "24-year-old patient presents 1 week s/p extraction of teeth 38 and 48" or "24-year-old patient presents *** s/p extraction of tooth 46". Then, only if adjuncts done at the surgery were mentioned, add " with " followed by them joined with commas and "and", using only these terms: socket preservation, alveoloplasty, primary closure, PRF; never add other items. Then, only if the surgery date was stated, add " on " followed by the date as stated. End the sentence with a full stop. Sentence 2: if the patient has any complaint, "Patient reports " followed by the complaints joined with commas and "and", using these terms where they fit and any other stated complaint in short clinical words: increasing pain, persistent swelling, bleeding, bad taste or odor, numbness, limited opening, food trapping; then a full stop. If no complaint was mentioned, always print "Patient reports doing well with minimal discomfort." Then, only if the onset or course of a complaint was described, such as "pain worsened on day 3", add one short sentence; else add nothing.)
[Pertinent negatives] (Print as a separate paragraph after a blank line. If the PATHWAY is ROUTINE, always print "Denies fever, chills, active bleeding, increasing swelling or altered sensation." and add any other symptom the patient denied before the final item. Otherwise print "Denies " followed only by the symptoms the patient actually denied, joined with commas and "or", using these terms where they fit: fever, chills, active bleeding, increasing swelling, altered sensation, difficulty swallowing, difficulty breathing; then a full stop; if no symptom was denied, omit this paragraph entirely. Never list a symptom here that the patient reports.)

EXAMINATION:
[Extraoral] (Always print "- Extraoral: " followed by exactly one of: "no facial swelling or lymphadenopathy", "mild residual facial swelling, no lymphadenopathy", or "significant facial swelling, " followed by the stated location and character, or "***" if not stated. Default to "no facial swelling or lymphadenopathy" unless facial swelling was found.)
[Opening] (Only include if mouth opening was examined or mentioned, else omit this line. Print "- MIO: " followed by "adequate (>40 mm)" or "limited, " followed by the stated measurement in mm, or "limited, *** mm" when no number was stated.)
[Sutures] (Only include if sutures were mentioned, else omit this line. Print "- Sutures: " followed by exactly one of: intact, resorbing, loose, none remaining. If sutures were removed today and their state was not described, print "intact".)
[Site lines] (Always print one line for every extracted tooth, in ascending FDI order: "- Site XX: " followed by that tooth's findings from SITE FINDINGS, joined with "; ", using exactly this wording:
HEALING WELL = "healing within normal limits, no erythema, purulence or exposed bone"
DRY SOCKET = "empty socket with exposed bone and fetid odor, tender to palpation"
INFECTION = "erythema and swelling with purulent drainage"
DELAYED HEALING = "delayed soft tissue healing for the postoperative interval"
SPICULE = "exposed bony spicule or sequestrum"
DEHISCENCE = "wound dehiscence"
RETAINED ROOT = "retained root fragment noted"
If the surgeon described a problem in different words, such as "socket open, no exposed bone", print their description as a short clinical phrase instead of the closest term. Then, only if another finding was stated for that site, such as "gingival operculum noted", add "; " and a short descriptive clinical phrase. For example "- Site 38: empty socket with exposed bone and fetid odor, tender to palpation" and "- Site 48: healing within normal limits, no erythema, purulence or exposed bone".)
[Neurosensory] (Print this line if MANDIBULAR POSTERIOR applies, or if NUMBNESS applies, or if sensation was examined or discussed; else omit it. If NUMBNESS applies, print "- Neurosensory: altered sensation of the " followed by the side (right, left or bilateral, else "***"), a space, the areas joined with commas and "and" using these terms where they fit: lower lip, chin, tongue, buccal gingiva (else "***"), then ", described as " followed by exactly one of numbness, tingling or burning pain (default numbness). Never print "intact" when NUMBNESS applies. Otherwise print "- Neurosensory: lip, chin and tongue sensation intact bilaterally to light touch".)
[Sinus] (Print this line if MAXILLARY POSTERIOR applies, or if the sinus or an oroantral communication was examined or discussed; else omit it. If OAC applies, print "- Sinus: oroantral communication present with positive nose-blow test, approximately " followed by the stated size and " mm", or "approximately *** mm" when no size was stated. Otherwise print "- Sinus: no clinical signs of oroantral communication".)
[Postop imaging] (Only include if a radiograph was taken or read at this visit, else omit this line. Print "- " followed by exactly one of "Periapical radiograph", "Panoramic radiograph" or "CBCT" ("***" if the type is unclear), then ": " and exactly one of: "extraction sockets without retained root fragments or foreign bodies" by default, or "retained root fragment at site " followed by the tooth number when a retained root was seen.)

ASSESSMENT:
[Assessment] (Write one short paragraph of sentences in this order, printing each only when it applies:
1. If any tooth is HEALING WELL: "Healing as expected following extraction of HEALED TEETH."
2. If any tooth has DRY SOCKET: "Alveolar osteitis of SITES OF DRY SOCKET."
3. If any tooth has INFECTION: "Postoperative infection of SITES OF INFECTION."
4. If any tooth has DELAYED HEALING: "Delayed healing of SITES OF DELAYED HEALING."
5. If any tooth has SPICULE: "Exposed bony spicule or sequestrum at SITES OF SPICULE."
6. If any tooth has DEHISCENCE: "Wound dehiscence at SITES OF DEHISCENCE."
7. If any tooth has RETAINED ROOT: "Retained root fragment at SITES OF RETAINED ROOT."
8. If NUMBNESS applies: "Postoperative neurosensory disturbance of the " followed by the same side and areas as the Neurosensory line, then a full stop.
9. If OAC applies: "Oroantral communication."
Decide yes or no for each numbered item against the SITE FINDINGS and REGIONS, and never skip one whose condition is met. For example "Healing as expected following extraction of tooth 48. Alveolar osteitis of site 38." Then, only if the surgeon stated another impression, add at most one short sentence; else add nothing.)

PLAN:
[Treatment today] (Only include if something was done at this visit, else omit this line. Print "- Treatment today: " followed by each that applies, in this order, joined with a space:
"Remaining sutures removed." if sutures were removed.
"Affected socket gently irrigated with sterile saline and a medicated dressing (Alvogyl) placed." if a socket was irrigated, packed or dressed. On the DRY SOCKET PATHWAY, print it if anything was done to the socket, even if only "packed it" or "rinsed it" was said.
"Loose bony sequestrum removed." if a sequestrum or loose bone was removed.
"Sharp bony spicule smoothed with a bone file under local anesthesia." if a spicule was smoothed or filed.
"Incision and drainage of the " followed by the stated location, or "***", then " performed under local anesthesia with purulent drainage obtained, and the wound irrigated." if an incision and drainage was done.
Any other procedure done today as one short clinical sentence.)
"- Postoperative instructions reviewed; continue soft diet and warm salt water rinses as directed."
[Analgesia] (If an analgesic was mentioned for after this visit, print "- Analgesia: " followed by "ibuprofen 600 mg PO q6h and acetaminophen 500 mg PO q6h as needed." for ibuprofen with acetaminophen, "acetaminophen 1000 mg PO q6h as needed, not to exceed 3 g per day." for acetaminophen alone, or the stated medication and regimen. If none was mentioned and the DRY SOCKET PATHWAY applies, print "- Analgesia: ibuprofen 600 mg PO q6h and acetaminophen 500 mg PO q6h as needed." Else omit this line.)
[Antibiotics] (Only include if an antibiotic was mentioned, else omit this line, on every pathway. Print "- Antibiotics: " followed by exactly one of: "complete the prescribed course." if the patient is continuing an antibiotic already prescribed; "amoxicillin 500 mg PO TID for 7 days." if amoxicillin was started; "clindamycin 300 mg PO QID for 7 days (penicillin allergy)." if clindamycin was started; or the stated antibiotic and regimen.)
[Chlorhexidine] (Print "- Chlorhexidine 0.12% rinse BID for 1 week." if chlorhexidine was mentioned or the INFECTION PATHWAY applies; else omit this line.)
[Nerve monitoring] (If NUMBNESS applies, always print verbatim: "- Neurosensory change reviewed with the patient; will monitor with serial neurosensory exams, with referral for microneurosurgical evaluation if no meaningful improvement by 3 months." Else omit this line.)
[Sinus precautions] (If OAC applies, always print verbatim: "- Sinus precautions reviewed: no nose blowing, sneeze with mouth open, no straws or smoking." Else omit this line.)
[Follow-up] (Always print "- Follow-up: " followed by the stated interval, such as "1 week." or "2 weeks."; when no interval was stated, print "2-3 days." if the DRY SOCKET PATHWAY applies, else "1 week." if the INFECTION PATHWAY applies, else "as needed.")
