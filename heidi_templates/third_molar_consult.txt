(GLOBAL INSTRUCTIONS: This is an oral and maxillofacial surgery consultation for third molars. Always follow the structure, order and wording of this template exactly. Write in a concise clinical style with short sentences and no narrative padding. Never add sections that are not in this template. Never use the patient's name; refer to them as "the patient". Use only plain keyboard characters: a hyphen instead of any dash, straight quotes, and no special symbols. Write every bullet as a plain text line that begins with a hyphen and a space; never use formatted list items.)

(CLINICAL TERMINOLOGY: The surgeon explains findings to the patient in plain English. Always translate these explanations back into clinical terminology in the note. For example: a gum flap over a tooth = operculum; a tooth stuck in the bone = bony impaction; fully in the bone = complete bony impaction; partly in the bone = partial bony impaction; covered only by gum = soft tissue impaction; tilted forward = mesioangular; tilted back = distoangular; lying sideways = horizontal; upright = vertical; the nerve = the inferior alveolar canal; roots still forming = incomplete root development; not enough room = insufficient space for eruption; infection around the tooth = pericoronitis; deep gum pockets = increased probing depths. Include findings the surgeon calls minor or not concerning, written as a short descriptive clinical phrase.)

(OBJECTIVE SECTIONS: EXAMINATION and IMAGING contain descriptive findings only. Never write judgments or interpretations in them, such as "not concerning", "benign-appearing", "likely reactive", "normal variant" or "no further workup needed"; drop the judgment and keep only the description, for example "mild erythema of the soft palate".)

(TOOTH NUMBERING: Always use FDI two-digit tooth numbers and never Universal numbers or the "#" symbol. Write "tooth 38", never "#17". Convert spoken descriptions: upper right wisdom tooth = 18, upper left = 28, lower left = 38, lower right = 48, upper right second molar = 17, upper left = 27, lower left = 37, lower right = 47. "8s" means third molars and "7s" means second molars. If the surgeon speaks Universal numbers, convert them: 1 = 18, 16 = 28, 17 = 38, 32 = 48, 2 = 17, 15 = 27, 18 = 37, 31 = 47. "All four wisdom teeth" means teeth 18, 28, 38 and 48.)

(TRANSCRIPT QUALITY: The transcript is often incomplete and poorly transcribed. Infer the intended clinical meaning from context, such as misheard drug names or dental terms, and make reasonable assumptions about what was discussed when the conversation clearly implies it. Never invent findings, medications, diagnoses or values that have no basis in the transcript, contextual notes or clinical note. Wherever this template says to print "***", print exactly three asterisks so the surgeon can complete it.)

(RADIOGRAPH READ: The surgeon may dictate a radiograph read in clinical terms before or after the patient is in the room, or type it into the contextual notes. That read is the authoritative source for the IMAGING section and for imaging-based statements in the ASSESSMENT, and it overrides anything said to the patient. If there is more than one read, the later one wins.)

(WORDING TOKENS: In the quoted wording below, replace these capitalized tokens. AGE = the patient's age in years, taken from the transcript, contextual notes or patient details; never drop it, and print *** in its place if it is not available, giving "***-year-old patient". TEETH = the third molars being evaluated and recommended for treatment, in ascending FDI order, written as "tooth 38" for one tooth or "teeth 38 and 48", "teeth 18, 28 and 38" for several. DECISION-MAKER = "the patient" by default; if a minor is accompanied by a parent, write "the patient's parent" followed by the parent's name in parentheses; if a legal guardian or healthcare power of attorney makes decisions, write "the patient's legal guardian" or "the patient's healthcare power of attorney" followed by their name in parentheses; use *** for any name not stated.)
