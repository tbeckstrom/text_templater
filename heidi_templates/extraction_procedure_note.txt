(GLOBAL INSTRUCTIONS: This is an oral and maxillofacial surgery procedure note for dental extractions. The surgeon dictates very briefly, often only the teeth, a few technique words and the anesthetic. Expanding a short dictation into the full note is the purpose of this template: always fill every unstated step with the default wording given below, and change or add only what the dictation states. Always follow the structure, order and wording of this template exactly. Write in a concise clinical style. Never add sections that are not in this template. Never use the patient's name; refer to them as "the patient". Use only plain keyboard characters: a hyphen instead of any dash, straight quotes, and no special symbols. Write every bullet as a plain text line that begins with a hyphen and a space; never use formatted list items.)

(CLINICAL TERMINOLOGY: Translate plain or shorthand speech into clinical terminology. For example: decay or a cavity = caries; can't be saved = nonrestorable; broken = fractured; only the root left or a root tip = retained root; loose with bone loss or gum disease = periodontitis with mobility; abscess or infection at the root = periapical abscess; dead nerve = pulpal necrosis; "ext" = extraction; "surg" = surgical; "flap" = full thickness mucoperiosteal flap; "trough" = buccal trough; "split" or "cut the roots" = roots sectioned; "cowhorn" = cowhorn forceps split; "alveo" = alveoloplasty; "socket graft" or "graft the socket" = socket preservation; "I and D" = incision and drainage; "lido" = 2% lidocaine with 1:100,000 epinephrine; "septo" or "arti" = 4% articaine with 1:100,000 epinephrine; "citanest" or "prilo" = 4% prilocaine plain; "marcaine" or "bupi" = 0.5% bupivacaine with 1:200,000 epinephrine; "carbo" or "mepi" = 3% mepivacaine plain; "carps" = carpules; "chromic" = chromic gut; "gas" or "laughing gas" = nitrous oxide; "OAC" = oroantral communication; "IAN" = inferior alveolar nerve.)

(TOOTH NUMBERING: Always use FDI two-digit tooth numbers and never Universal numbers or the "#" symbol. Write "tooth 36", never "#19". The first digit is the quadrant: upper right = 1, upper left = 2, lower left = 3, lower right = 4. The second digit is the position from the midline: central incisor = 1, lateral incisor = 2, canine = 3, first premolar = 4, second premolar = 5, first molar = 6, second molar = 7, third molar = 8. For example, the lower left first molar = 36 and the upper right canine = 13. If the surgeon speaks Universal numbers, convert them: 1 = 18, 2 = 17, 3 = 16, 4 = 15, 5 = 14, 6 = 13, 7 = 12, 8 = 11, 9 = 21, 10 = 22, 11 = 23, 12 = 24, 13 = 25, 14 = 26, 15 = 27, 16 = 28, 17 = 38, 18 = 37, 19 = 36, 20 = 35, 21 = 34, 22 = 33, 23 = 32, 24 = 31, 25 = 41, 26 = 42, 27 = 43, 28 = 44, 29 = 45, 30 = 46, 31 = 47, 32 = 48. Always list teeth in ascending FDI order.)

(TRANSCRIPT QUALITY: The transcript is short and may be poorly transcribed. Infer the intended clinical meaning from context, such as misheard drug names or dental terms. Never invent numbers such as carpule counts, anesthesia time, blood pressure or graft lot numbers; those print "***" unless stated. Wherever this template says to print "***", print exactly three asterisks so the surgeon can complete it.)

(SIMPLE OR SURGICAL: Classify every extracted tooth. A tooth is SIMPLE only if the dictation clearly says so for it, for example "simple", "forceps", "just elevated", "came right out", "easy" or "luxated and delivered". Every other extracted tooth is SURGICAL, including any tooth with no technique stated at all. A tooth that was first attempted simply and then needed a flap, bone removal or sectioning is SURGICAL and "converted".)

(SEDATION: IV SEDATION applies if IV sedation, IV general anesthesia or "IVGA" was used. NITROUS applies if nitrous oxide was used. Local anesthesia only is the default.)
